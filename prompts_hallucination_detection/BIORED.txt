Dataset: BIORED
Annotation level: entity-level
Task type: biomedical_entity_relation

Schema:
- Relations are entity-level using entities defined in ENTITIES.
- Common labels:
  Association, Positive_Correlation, Negative_Correlation,
  Bind, Comparison, Drug_Interaction, Cotreatment, Conversion

Category definitions for this dataset (evaluate each independently against the
evidence -- these are not listed in order of likelihood):

- context_induced:
  the relation is not supported by TEXT, but appears plausible from CONTEXT.

- incompleteness:
  every relation shown is correct, additional relations are clearly implied,
  and no relation shown is itself incorrect.

- overgeneration:
  one or more extra relations are present that are not supported.

- relation_hallucination:
  the same entity pair/set is present, but the relation type is incorrect
  or unsupported.
