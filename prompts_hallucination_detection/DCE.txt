Dataset: DCE
Annotation level: mention-level
Task type: drug_combination_relation

Schema:
- Each relation connects two or more drug mentions from ENTITIES.
- Valid labels:
  POS, NEG, COMB

Category definitions for this dataset (evaluate each independently against the
evidence -- these are not listed in order of likelihood):

- context_induced:
  the combination is not supported by TEXT, but appears plausible from CONTEXT.

- incompleteness:
  the combination shown is valid, additional drugs or relations are clearly
  implied, and no relation shown is itself incorrect.

- overgeneration:
  extra unsupported drug combinations or relations are present.

- relation_hallucination:
  the same drug combination is present, but the label is incorrect.
