Dataset: DDI
Annotation level: mention-level
Task type: drug_drug_interaction

Schema:
- Each relation connects two medication mentions using roles e1 and e2.
- Medication mentions may include DRUG or BRAND types.
- Valid labels:
  MECHANISM, EFFECT, ADVISE, INT

Important interpretation rules:
- Do NOT treat simple co-occurrence, monitoring, dosage, or measurement as
  sufficient evidence of interaction.
- A valid interaction must be explicitly or strongly supported by the evidence.

Category definitions for this dataset (evaluate each independently against the
evidence -- these are not listed in order of likelihood):

- context_induced:
  the relation is NOT supported by TEXT, but is strongly suggested by CONTEXT.

- incompleteness:
  the existing relations appear correct, additional interactions are clearly
  implied, and no relation shown is itself incorrect.

- overgeneration:
  one or more extra unsupported drug-drug interactions are present.

- relation_hallucination:
  the same drug pair is present, but the interaction type is incorrect or
  unsupported.

If a case could plausibly fit more than one category, choose based on which
discrepancy is most central to why this record differs from what the evidence
actually supports -- not by defaulting to any one category as a fallback.
