Dataset: CHEMPROT
Annotation level: mention-level
Task type: chemical_gene_relation

Schema:
- Each relation connects two entity mentions from ENTITIES.
- Valid pairing:
  CHEMICAL (arg1) -> GENE (arg2)
- Valid labels:
  CPR:3, CPR:4, CPR:5, CPR:6, CPR:9

Category definitions for this dataset (evaluate each independently against the
evidence -- these are not listed in order of likelihood):

- context_induced:
  the relation is not supported by TEXT, but appears plausible from CONTEXT.

- incompleteness:
  every relation shown is correct, additional chemical-gene relations are
  clearly implied, and no relation shown is itself incorrect.

- overgeneration:
  one or more extra unsupported chemical-gene relations are present.

- relation_hallucination:
  the same chemical-gene pair is present, but the relation label is
  incorrect or unsupported.
