Dataset: DCE
Annotation level: mention-level
Task type: drug_combination_relation
Category: context_induced

SCHEMA CONSTRAINTS (enforced; violations are rejected)
1. A relation has TWO OR MORE arguments (a drug combination).
2. Every role is "span". There is no arg1/arg2 distinction.
3. Valid labels only: POS, COMB, NEG.
4. Every argument must be a DRUG entity from the entities list.
5. No entity may appear twice in one combination.
6. Relations are non-directional; argument order carries no meaning.

WHAT MAKES A GOOD DCE CONTEXT-INDUCED INSTANCE
DCE is the one corpus where context_text is a genuine paragraph from the
source abstract rather than a summary, and roughly two thirds of gold
relations need that paragraph to determine efficacy. That makes it the
natural home for this category.

Assert a combination the focal sentence does not support, then rewrite the
paragraph so the combination looks efficacious (or non-efficacious) to a
reader working from context alone. Efficacy labels are the consequential
ones: POS and NEG invert each other and would change a treatment choice.
COMB is the weakest signal; prefer POS or NEG.

REMINDERS
- The combination you assert must not match any gold combination.
- Keep the arity realistic: two or three drugs, matching how the corpus
  describes combinations.
- Return only the new combination, not the gold ones.
- context_text is required and must differ from current_context_text.
- Return JSON only.
